How it works

Free to start.
Simple from there.

No long setup and no subscription to begin. Connect a provider you trust and start talking.

  1. 1

    Pick a provider

    Start with one that offers a free tier, like Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter — or run a local model through Ollama or LM Studio. Free tiers have their own limits.

  2. 2

    Connect it

    Add it once in Settings. MetaIoid checks the connection and tells you plainly whether it’s working.

  3. 3

    Start talking

    Type or speak. Leave the model on Auto, or choose Fast, Balanced, Smart or Vision when you want to.

Privacy and control

It’s yours.
It behaves that way.

No surprises. MetaIoid tells you what it’s using, what it’s doing and whether something isn’t working.

Your AI

You choose the provider and the model. Leave it on Auto, or pick exactly the one you want.

Your keys

Provider credentials are stored encrypted and kept out of logs, links and responses.

Your costs

A free-only setting keeps you on free models, and falling back to another model is a switch you control.

Your view

Connection status is shown honestly. If a provider is down or unset, MetaIoid says so.
